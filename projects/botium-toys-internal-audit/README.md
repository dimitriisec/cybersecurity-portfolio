# Botium Toys Internal IT Audit

This project simulates an internal IT audit for Botium Toys, a fictional U.S. company expanding its online presence. As the company grows, the IT manager is concerned about maintaining compliance, protecting critical assets, and ensuring secure business operations. The audit follows the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF) and evaluates existing controls, risks, and compliance posture.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Review the IT manager’s scope, goals, and risk assessment report  
- Evaluate existing security controls  
- Assess compliance with PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC frameworks  
- Identify gaps and risks  
- Provide actionable recommendations to strengthen the security posture

## 🛡️ Summary of Findings

Botium Toys lacks several critical security controls, including Least Privilege enforcement, separation of duties, encryption, and secure password management. Compliance gaps exist across PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC requirements, primarily due to insufficient access controls, lack of data classification, and missing incident response procedures.
