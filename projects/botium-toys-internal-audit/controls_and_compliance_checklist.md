## 📝 Recommendations

Botium Toys must strengthen its security posture by implementing several key controls, including Least Privilege, separation of duties, encryption, and secure password management. Compliance gaps should be addressed by improving access controls, documenting privacy processes, and classifying assets to determine additional required controls.
